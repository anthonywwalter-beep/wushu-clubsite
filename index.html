Welcome to UO Wushu!

No experience necessary. Just interest.

Who We Are

UO Wushu is a group of students who train and perform modern Chinese martial arts. Most people who join have never tried Wushu before, and that’s totally normal. We teach everything from basic stances and kicks to full routines, and we help each other improve at our own pace.

Read more

If you’re curious about martial arts, want to get active, or just want to try something new with a friendly group of people, you’re welcome here. We’re a mix of beginners, athletes, performers, and people who just enjoy learning cool movement skills together.

What We Do

We meet each week to practice Wushu forms, kicks, jumps, and performance skills. A typical session includes warm-ups, learning new techniques, and working on short routines. No experience is needed, we'll teach you everything step-by-step.

Read more

Throughout the year, we also host beginner workshops, travel to compete, and put together performances for campus events. Some members choose to train for competitions, while others come simply for fun, fitness, and community. No matter your goal, you’ll have people supporting you.
